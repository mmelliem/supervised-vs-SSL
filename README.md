# supervised-vs-SSL
Comparing PaSST and BYOL-A on music genre and word classification tasks to compare the effectiveness of supervised and self-supervised learning objectives. I performed PCA and ANOVA on their intermediate layer embeddings to see how the models specialize for different audio tasks, and compared their accuracy after training a classifier on their final embeddings.
